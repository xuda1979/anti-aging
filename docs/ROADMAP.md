# From a reproducible pilot to a testable mechanism

**David Xu, PhD; Peter Lee, PhD, MD; and Zhimin Zheng, PhD**  
21 October 2026 — proposed research, subject to experimental leadership, resources and rights review.

The long-term ambition is comprehensive reversal of cellular biological aging. No executed work in this repository demonstrates that outcome. A chronological-age predictor is an analysis component. Moving its output is not proof of restoring function, repairing damage, or preserving cell identity.

## Executed work

The first small-scale study trains a regularized linear model and a compact neural model on freely downloadable, processed human fibroblast RNA-seq data. Frozen sample splits, fold-specific transformations, a training-median baseline, shuffled-label diagnostics, per-sample predictions, saved checkpoints, tests and an independent numerical verifier make the result inspectable. The study is limited to one public cohort; its holdout is not external validation. See `results/pilot_20261021_v2/REPORT.md` and `verification/REPORT.md`.

A public association-based coefficient list is a model inspection artifact. It is not a list of validated rejuvenation targets and must not be used to prescribe a treatment.

## Technical architecture and disciplined scope

The proposed platform may combine RNA, methylation, chromatin accessibility, imaging and perturbation models. Multi-modal encoders, regulatory sequence models and virtual-cell predictors are candidates, not already trained or validated capabilities of this release. Unpaired modalities require an explicit alignment assumption. Causal identification needs interventions, controls and adequate support; an observational predictor cannot supply it alone. Literature language models can help collect hypotheses and audit provenance, but do not replace original-source review or experiments. Bayesian selection can allocate experiments once uncertainties and utilities are calibrated.

Foundation-model training is not justified by 111 adult samples. The compact pilot establishes an inexpensive baseline; pretraining or larger architectures should be adopted only when a matched benchmark shows incremental value. Independent function and identity guardrails must remain outside the optimization target to detect clock gaming.

## Business milestones

The existing white paper and financial model describe an **illustrative $8 million, 24-month discovery program**, not a committed raise or validated forecast. The first financing decision should resolve scientific leadership and availability, company jurisdiction, ownership and employment conflicts, a concrete therapeutic use case, prospective evidence and customer discovery. No institutional sponsorship, clinical results, proprietary validated target or team role commitments have been established. Public code and public-data reanalysis alone do not establish defensible therapeutic IP or commercial demand.

Potential investors, research organizations, employers and biotech companies can inspect the same nonconfidential evidence. A collaboration or employment discussion is separate from an investment commitment. No terms, spending commitment, IP assignment or role acceptance is made by this release.
