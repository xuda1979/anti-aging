# Earlier manuscript archive

`Cellular_Age_Resetting_AI_2026-10-13.pdf` is the supplied 13 October 2026 research-framework manuscript, originally credited to Da Xu. It reports a 34-sample/three-donor treatment-classification pilot and synthetic experiments. It is preserved as a historical source referenced by the subsequent business documents.

Its companion `CellClockAI_Paper_Code_Results.zip` was subsequently recovered on 21 October 2026. All 59 original scientific files, including a separately supplied manuscript PDF and its editable source, are preserved unchanged under [`legacy/CellClockAI/`](../../legacy/CellClockAI/). The supplied PDF here and the PDF in that archive have distinct byte hashes; both are retained with provenance rather than silently treated as identical. The original code is MIT-licensed and original manuscript/figures are CC BY 4.0; third-party data retain their own rights.

A fresh CPU replay reproduced all 11 compared result files byte for byte. Its public treatment classifier and six-scenario synthetic benchmark remain **association and simulator evidence, not demonstrated rejuvenation**. Six original unit tests passed, and the optional Torch test was skipped; the historical graph smoke was not newly executed. See the [reproduction record](../../legacy/REPRODUCTION.md) and [independent verification](../../legacy/verification/REPORT.md). The earlier source-unavailable note has been corrected.

The manuscript names an affiliation; this repository and the proposed venture assert **no institutional sponsorship or endorsement**. The current project authors supplied by the initiator are David Xu, PhD; Peter Lee, PhD, MD; and Zhimin Zheng, PhD. Their founder roles, availability and contribution approvals remain to be agreed.

The independently inspected new study is the GSE113957 healthy-adult chronological-age reanalysis under `results/`, with a separate protocol and original-source attribution. It is not a replication of the earlier treatment-classification pilot.
